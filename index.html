Who am I

I'm Aditya, a 3rd semester Informatics student at Horizon University Indonesia, Karawang. Started coding as a curiosity, ended up hooked. Now I'm exploring web dev, building games, and diving into UI/UX.

Believe it or not, I actually enjoy debugging—there's something deeply satisfying about finally solving an error that's been haunting you for 2 hours. Most of my projects start with "I wonder if I can build this?" and end up as "well, let's just try it and see."
